1. About this policy

This policy explains how Jump Tower handles game information and how to contact Tervixa Games about privacy. It also explains what happens when you visit this policy website or send us an email.

2. Gameplay information stored on your device

Jump Tower uses Unity PlayerPrefs, a local storage mechanism, to remember your progress between sessions. This includes:

  • Your best score and coin balance.
  • Owned and equipped character skins and background themes.
  • Music, sound and haptics preferences.
  • Daily Reward progress, including the last claim date and reward day.
  • Lifetime Stats, such as jumps, games played, coins earned, platform achievements, rocks dodged and gameplay time.

These records support gameplay, rewards, customization and settings. They are not a user account. The reviewed gameplay code does not upload these records to a Tervixa Games server or provide a developer-operated cloud-save service.

Android or your device provider may separately back up and restore app data according to your device settings. This can include restoring local progress after reinstalling the app.

3. Accounts, advertising and purchases

Jump Tower does not require an account or login. The app has no advertising and no in-app purchases for real money. Its Shop uses virtual coins earned through gameplay and Daily Rewards.

The reviewed app does not include forms asking for your name, email address or date of birth, or features requesting access to your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone.

4. Unity, diagnostics and network behavior

Jump Tower is built with Unity. Unity Analytics, Unity Engine Diagnostics and legacy cloud crash reporting are disabled in the current project configuration. Android Engine Diagnostics is explicitly disabled in its build profile.

No advertising, attribution, Firebase or real-money purchasing integration was found in the project audit. Some installed Unity components have networking capabilities, and the Android application currently retains Internet permission. Those capabilities alone do not establish that information is transmitted.

Review of the game code found no game-authored network requests or transmission of local progress. The existing Android App Bundle was built before the explicit diagnostics-off settings were saved. Those project settings therefore do not establish the behavior of that bundle. Network behavior of the Android application and its included SDKs has not yet been verified on a device. We do not claim that the application never collects or transmits data.

Google Play and Android may process information independently when you download, install, update, back up or use an Android application. Their processing is governed by their own policies and your platform settings.

5. Retention and deletion

Local progress remains in app storage between runs; restarting a run does not erase it. You can remove local saves using Android Settings → Apps → Jump Tower → Storage → Clear storage (wording varies by device), or by uninstalling the app. This removes local progress, earned coins, cosmetic unlocks, settings, Daily Reward history and Stats.

Copies in device or cloud backups may remain or be restored. Manage those separately through your device or backup provider. Tervixa Games cannot remotely erase data stored only on your device or in backups controlled by another provider.

If you email tervixagames@gmail.com, Google handles your email address, message and any attachments as our email provider so we can respond. Only the developer accesses this support mailbox, and Google two-factor authentication is enabled. Resolved support emails and attachments will be deleted within 90 days after resolution. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. Please send only information needed for your request, and never send passwords or payment details. This retention commitment concerns the support correspondence we manage; Google-managed backups and service logs are governed by Google’s own policies and are not a promise of deletion from every provider system.

6. Children's privacy

Jump Tower is intended to include children aged 9–12 as well as older players. It does not require children to register, provide contact details or create a profile to play. Gameplay progress is stored as described above, and the game has no advertising or real-money purchases.

Parents or guardians can manage or remove local app data through the device settings. Please have an adult contact us about a child's privacy concern and avoid sending unnecessary information about the child. The Android verification limitation described in section 4 also applies to children's use of the app.

7. This website and external services

This website uses GitHub Pages hosting. Its source includes no analytics scripts, advertising, third-party fonts, embedded media, forms, cookies or browser-storage code.

GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security, including visitors who are not signed in. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting an external policy link or sending an email also involves the relevant browser, website or email provider.

8. Policy updates

We will update this policy when the app's data practices change and show the revised effective date on this page.
